# What the catalogue says about this collection

Written 2026-09-06, from resolving the owner's list against tercerafundacion.net.
A dated record: it is what the catalogue held on that day.

The list as given had 45 lines under four headings, six of which were repeats
across headings (`El temor de la Fundación`, `El jardín de Rama`,
`La ciudad al final del tiempo`, `Criópolis`, and two more), so the shelf is
**39 distinct books**. Each was matched against every catalogued edition of its
title, then the match was argued against by a second reader. One pick out of 39
was overturned.

## The headings are a publishing house, not an imprint

The three headings in the list turn out to be the catalogue's own two fields,
`Colección` and `Editorial`:

| Heading as written | Colección | Editorial |
|---|---|---|
| Nova ciencia ficción | Nova | Ediciones B |
| Ciencia Ficción. Editorial Zeta Bolsillo | Ciencia Ficción | Zeta Bolsillo |
| Ciencia Ficción. Editorial B de Bolsillo | Ciencia Ficción | B de Bolsillo |

That made the match checkable, and it turned up the main finding: **17 of the 39
books are not in the imprint the list assigns them to.** Sixteen sit under
"B de Bolsillo" but were published by Ediciones B under three earlier imprints,
and none of those titles has a B de Bolsillo printing in the catalogue at all.

The reading: **B de Bolsillo is the current name of the house's pocket line, and
the list uses it for anything from that house that is not obviously Nova.** VIB
is the pocket line it replaced, and Steelheart is the reverse case, filed under
Zeta Bolsillo when Zeta had already become B de Bolsillo by 2015.

The shelf keeps the owner's own labels, because that is how the books physically
stand. Switch **Group** to **Collection** to see what the catalogue says instead.

## One title, more than one physical book

- **Proteo** (Nova 85) is an omnibus. Its contents are `El ascenso de Proteo`
  (pp. 11 to 166) and the whole of `Proteo desencadenado` (pp. 167 to 360). One
  spine, two novels. If a separate `Proteo desencadenado` is on the shelf, it is
  a different record and nothing under the query "Proteo" would have found it.
- **Mapas en un espejo** exists both as the 919-page omnibus chosen here and as
  a five-volume pocket set, each volume separately subtitled. The heading points
  at the five-volume set, but the list has one entry, so the omnibus was taken.
  If the shelf holds five books, four are missing from it.
- **Ilión** was split into two volumes in Spanish and later reissued complete in
  one. Both halves are on the shelf, which is consistent with the split edition.
- **El apagón** is the first half of a two-part work; the second is
  `Cese de alerta`, and it is not on the list.

## Worth an eye on the physical copy

These picks are defensible but not certain, and only the book in hand settles them.

- **El dragón en el mar.** No B de Bolsillo printing exists. The two candidates
  are Nova 40 (sep 1991, 15x22 cm, Juan Giménez cover) and VIB 23 (mar 1993,
  11x17,5 cm pocket). Nova 40 was taken, on the grounds that the "bolsillo"
  heading holds trade editions elsewhere in the list and so cannot be read as
  meaning pocket. If the copy is the small one with the stock-photo cover, it is
  record 555.
- **Criópolis.** B de Bolsillo printed it twice in the same collection, 2013 and
  a 2019 reissue marked `Saga Vorkosigan 15`. Only the 2013 has a scan.
- **La Tierra desprevenida.** Two printings, 2014 with flaps and a slightly
  smaller 2019 without. Both have scans; the older was taken.
- **Mareas de luz**, **Serpiente del sueño**, **Hijos de la mente** and others
  have a taller Nova and a shorter pocket printing. The cover art differs, so
  the cover is the quickest way to tell.

## Catalogue quirks found along the way

- Some stored titles carry a trailing `EN VENTA`, a for-sale marker rather than
  part of the title. Stripped on read.
- `Rama revelada` appears twice (51943 and 74290) under one ISBN, differing only
  in page count and in which of "Ciencia Ficción" and "B de Bolsillo" is recorded
  as collection versus publisher. `Los viajes de Tuf` and `Steelheart` are
  likewise entered twice.
- Several records leave the author field empty; identification then rests on the
  translator, the collection number and the page count.
- The catalogue files `Elantris` under Fantasía even though its collection is
  named Ciencia Ficción.

## Gaps the catalogue can name

Browse shows volumes numbered in a series already on the shelf. The clearest is
**Chanur**: four of the five are here, and `El legado de Chanur` is not.
