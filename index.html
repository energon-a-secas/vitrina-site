Stand your science fiction on a real shelf

Vitrina draws your Spanish science fiction paperbacks as spines on a shelf, at the height the catalogue records for each book and a thickness worked out from its page count, using the old cover and spine scans kept by La Tercera Fundación.

See the demo shelf Start your own shelf

  • Scan or type an ISBN

    Point the camera at the barcode or type the number. It looks the book up in the collections catalogued so far, on your machine, and offers every edition that shares the number.

  • See what you are missing

    Draw a whole collection with your copies lit and the rest dimmed, so a gap in the numbering is a gap on the shelf.

  • Keep it in your browser

    Your shelf is stored on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and an export hands you your shelf as a file.

Cover and spine scans come from La Tercera Fundación, a volunteer catalogue of Spanish-language science fiction. Small copies of the demo shelf's books are kept here so it loads quickly; everything else is loaded straight from the catalogue.
